Play heads-up No-Limit Texas Hold’em against a GTO bot. The bot follows Nash equilibrium strategy: it uses pot-odds-based calling frequencies, polarized betting ranges (value bets + balanced bluffs), and mixed strategies to be unexploitable. It cannot see your cards and makes all decisions based solely on its own hand equity vs. a random opponent range. Blinds are 1/2, starting stacks 200. Play 10 rounds.
